# Option B, fewer parts: a mains Zigbee CCT driver

One box instead of PSU + controller: a **230 V Zigbee constant-current CCT driver** (Tuya `TS0502B` type, e.g.
DC 72–185 V, 260 mA, 2 channels with common +) driving **high-voltage 3-pin chandelier strip**, for example the
[Electro Lab 3 PIN 5B10C/5B20C, 85 UAH/m](https://prom.ua/ua/p3156734177-svetodiodnaya-lenta-pin.html) (3000 + 6500 K, 60 V per metre).
It works in Z2M ([TS0502B](https://www.zigbee2mqtt.io/devices/TS0502B.html)) and could cost about 600–900 UAH, but
**no Ukrainian seller of the Zigbee driver was found** (AliExpress/Taobao only), the strip carries up to ~185 V DC on
exposed pads, and cheap drivers are often not isolated from mains. Not recommended.
